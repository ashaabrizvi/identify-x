What is Xaily?

Xaily is a thinking first experience built around deduction, not memory. Each day, you are presented with a single cryptic question, the same one for everyone.

There are no options, no shortcuts, and no pressure. Only clues that reward patience and reasoning.

Why Xaily exists

Most digital experiences are designed for speed, stimulation, and constant reaction. Xaily exists to slow that rhythm down.

It offers a quiet space to think clearly, reflect carefully, and engage with ideas through reasoning rather than urgency.

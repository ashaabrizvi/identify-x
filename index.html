Identify X

A daily cryptic deduction game that makes you think.

What is Identify X?

Identify X is a daily thinking challenge. You get one carefully written, cryptic question per day. No trivia. No guessing. Just reasoning.

Example

“X has become synonymous with genius, often used to describe exceptional intelligence. Yet during their time, recognition was limited. Their work reshaped how we understand reality itself.”

How it works

  • One global X per day
  • Same question for everyone
  • Cryptic hints revealed gradually
  • Free forever
